QARSHI REMITTANCE

PRIVACY POLICY • LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 16, 2026

Qarshi Remittance Technologies Inc. ("Qarshi", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Qarshi mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and securing your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you utilize our cross-border money transfer platform.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate secure international money transfers from the United States to mobile wallets and banking systems in East Africa—specifically covering Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, and Djibouti—we collect several categories of information:

  • Identity Information: Full Name, Date of Birth, and Government Identification (such as a Passport or Driver’s License image).
  • Compliance Metadata: Last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (SSN) to execute automated Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulatory verifications.
  • Contact & Account Data: Email Address, Physical US Address, Phone Number, and Secure App Login Credentials.
  • Financial Details: Bank account tokenized references pulled via secure integration networks (such as Plaid or Stripe). We never store raw banking routing or account numbers on our local databases.
  • Transaction Record Data: Specific transaction amounts, converted exchange rates, timestamps, and recipient details. This includes receiving digital networks across our active regional corridors:
    • Ethiopia: Telebirr, CBE Birr, AwashBirr, and mobile telephone numbers.
    • Somalia: EVC Plus (Hormuud), Zaad Wallet (Telesom), and mobile telephone numbers.
    • Djibouti: Evatis, D-Money, and mobile telephone numbers.
    • Kenya: M-Pesa (Safaricom), Airtel Money, and mobile telephone numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize your collected personal data strictly to maintain compliance infrastructure and provide our services:

  • To process cross-border money transfers securely.
  • To comply with the United States FinCEN and partner central bank regulations. This includes reporting mandates governed by the National Bank of Ethiopia, the Central Bank of Kenya, the Central Bank of Somalia, and the Central Bank of Djibouti, alongside mandatory US Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) compliance guidelines.
  • To monitor and eliminate transaction structuring, identity fraud, and cybersecurity threats.
  • To transmit automated notifications and receipt logs regarding transfer completion metrics.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party API Processing

Qarshi handles your information securely and will never sell your personal data. To provide regional remittance pipelines, we securely pass tokenized data parameters to our integrated, licensed partners:

  • Payment Infrastructure: Stripe and Plaid for USD funding collection.
  • Remittance Infrastructure: Our designated licensed Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) platforms and international payout network operators to transmit local funds or mobile digital currency straight to active regional mobile networks.

4. Data Security and Encryption

All data transmitted within the Qarshi application framework is fully encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL) and Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256) network architectures. Your identity documentation files are isolated in restricted storage clouds.

5. Your Rights and Retention

In accordance with federal and international financial regulations, structural transaction logs and identity parameters cannot be immediately removed upon request. This data must be securely retained for a minimum statutory period of five (5) years to fulfill anti-structuring and anti-terrorist financing compliance reporting mandates.
