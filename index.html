1. Scope and Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Qarshi Pay App ("Qarshi," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information when you use our mobile application, website, customer support, or related services. Qarshi Pay is designed for eligible U.S. customers who send money to supported recipients and mobile wallets through approved remittance, funding, identity-verification, screening, and payout partners.

This policy applies only to Qarshi-controlled services. A bank, Plaid, identity-verification provider, licensed remittance provider, payout partner, wallet operator, or other third party may process information under its own privacy notice.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the requirements of our partners, we may collect:

  • Identity and eligibility information: Name, date of birth, citizenship or residency information, government-issued identification, selfie or liveness results, Social Security number or portions of it, and identity-verification results.
  • Contact and account information: Email address, mobile number, residential address, login credentials, authentication records, language preferences, and account settings.
  • Funding and financial-connection information: Bank name, account type, masked account details, account identifiers or tokens, connection status, funding method, payment-authorization status, and return or chargeback information.
  • Transfer and recipient information: Send amount, fees, exchange rate, amount received, corridor, recipient name, recipient mobile number, wallet or payout method, timestamps, delivery estimate, transaction status, receipt, and reference number.
  • Compliance and risk information: Identity-screening, sanctions and watchlist results, fraud indicators, transaction-monitoring alerts, source-of-funds information, case notes, eligibility decisions, and records required by law or regulated partners.
  • Device, usage, and security information: IP address, device and app identifiers, operating system, app version, login activity, diagnostic logs, crash reports, approximate location inferred from IP, security events, and interaction data.
  • Communications: Messages, support requests, complaints, survey responses, and records of notices or consents you provide.

3. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you when you create an account, verify your identity, connect a funding method, enter recipient details, request a transfer, or contact support.
  • Automatically from your device and use of the app, including security, diagnostic, and interaction records.
  • From providers and partners such as Plaid, banks, payment processors, identity and sanctions-screening providers, licensed remittance providers, payout networks, and mobile-wallet operators.
  • From public, regulatory, fraud-prevention, or legally authorized sources when needed for compliance and security.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and support your Qarshi Pay account.
  • Verify identity and eligibility and perform Know Your Customer, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, fraud, and security checks.
  • Connect or verify funding methods and request payment authorization through the applicable provider.
  • Calculate, review, submit, process, track, reconcile, and support transfers and related receipts.
  • Display corridor-specific wallets, exchange rates, fees, recipient amounts, delivery estimates, and transaction states supplied or confirmed by approved partners.
  • Send service, security, compliance, transaction, support, and policy communications.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate incidents, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect customers, recipients, partners, and the public.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, recordkeeping, audit, reporting, and partner obligations.
  • Improve accessibility, reliability, performance, and customer experience using appropriately limited or aggregated information where feasible.

5. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Funding and account-connection providers, such as Plaid, banks, ACH providers, card or payment processors, and financial institutions used to connect or fund a transfer.
  • Licensed remittance and payout partners responsible for regulated money movement, foreign exchange, settlement, delivery, error resolution, and required disclosures.
  • Identity, compliance, and fraud providers supporting identity verification, document validation, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, device intelligence, and case management.
  • Technology and operations providers supporting hosting, communications, customer support, analytics, logging, cybersecurity, and professional services under appropriate restrictions.
  • Recipient-facing providers, including participating banks, payout networks, and supported mobile-wallet operators needed to deliver or troubleshoot a transfer.
  • Authorities and protected parties when disclosure is required or permitted by law, legal process, regulatory request, audit, investigation, safety need, fraud prevention, or protection of rights.
  • Business-transaction participants in connection with a proposed or completed financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

Qarshi does not sell personal information for money. If a future practice is treated as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under applicable law, we will provide any notice and choice required before using that practice.

6. Plaid and Connected Financial Accounts

If you choose to connect a financial account using Plaid, Plaid may collect and process information under its own privacy policy and present its own consent flow. Qarshi receives only the information and permissions needed for the enabled feature, subject to configuration and applicable law. We seek to use tokenized or limited account information rather than your online-banking password.

A successful account connection does not by itself mean that a payment has been authorized, a transfer has been submitted, or funds have been delivered. Disconnecting an account does not require deletion of records that Qarshi or its regulated partners must retain for transactions, security, disputes, or legal compliance.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, regulatory, audit, tax, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, and partner obligations. Retention periods vary by record type, account status, transaction history, jurisdiction, and the licensed provider's role.

Certain transaction, identity, compliance, and investigation records may need to be retained for five years or another period required by law or a regulated partner. Other records may be deleted or de-identified sooner. A deletion request does not override lawful retention duties.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, such as encryption in transit where appropriate, access controls, authentication, logging, vendor oversight, secure development practices, and incident-response procedures. No system or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may be able to:

  • Access, correct, or update certain account information.
  • Request deletion of eligible information or closure of your account.
  • Request a copy of certain information or details about how it is processed.
  • Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Manage marketing messages while continuing to receive essential service, security, and transaction communications.
  • Appeal a denied privacy request where applicable.

To submit a request, email support@qarshiapp.com. We may verify your identity before responding and may deny or limit a request when permitted or required by law.

10. Children and Eligibility

Qarshi Pay is not intended for children under 13, and the money-transfer service is intended only for users who meet the age and eligibility requirements established by Qarshi and its licensed partners. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from a child under 13 without legally required authorization. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.

11. International Processing

Because Qarshi Pay is designed for cross-border transfers, information may be processed in the United States and in countries associated with approved recipients, payout providers, mobile wallets, technology providers, or regulators. Privacy and data-protection laws may differ across jurisdictions. We and our partners will use safeguards required for the applicable processing.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

The app may link to or interact with services controlled by other organizations. Their privacy notices, terms, security practices, availability, and data handling are their responsibility. Review the relevant notice before authorizing a third party to collect or receive your information.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product, partners, legal requirements, or data practices change. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required. Material product changes will not be treated as active until the corresponding app, partner, and operational configuration is enabled.
