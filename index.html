1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Qarshi Pay App pre-launch project ("Qarshi," "we," "us," or "our") may collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information through its application, website, testing environment, waiting list, or support channels. Before launch, this section must be reconciled with the registered legal entity, actual production data inventory, approved providers, and applicable notices.

Information we may collect
  • Identity and eligibility: name, date of birth, address, identification number, government identification, selfie or liveness result, and verification outcome.
  • Contact and account: email, mobile number, credentials, authentication events, language, and settings.
  • Financial connection and funding: institution name, account type, masked details, tokens, funding status, authorization, returns, and chargebacks.
  • Transfer and recipient: sender and recipient details, amount, fees, rate, recipient amount, payout method, status, timestamps, receipt, and reference.
  • Compliance and risk: identity, sanctions, fraud, transaction-monitoring, source-of-funds, case, and eligibility records.
  • Device, usage, and security: IP address, device/app identifiers, version, logs, diagnostics, approximate IP location, and security events.
  • Communications: support messages, complaints, requests, consents, and notices.
How information may be collected and used

Information may be obtained directly from you, automatically from the app or device, from approved banks and service providers, and from lawful fraud-prevention, public, or regulatory sources. It may be used to create and secure accounts; verify identity and eligibility; perform sanctions, AML, and fraud controls; connect funding methods; calculate and process approved transfers; provide receipts and support; resolve errors and complaints; protect rights and security; and meet legal, regulatory, audit, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.

How information may be disclosed

Information may be disclosed as necessary to approved funding institutions, bank-link providers, licensed remittance providers, payout institutions and wallets, identity and screening providers, hosting and support vendors, professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement or other lawful recipients, and transaction participants subject to appropriate restrictions. Qarshi does not sell personal information for money.

Connected financial accounts

If an approved bank-link service such as Plaid is enabled, it may provide its own consent and privacy notice. Qarshi should receive only the information and permissions required for the enabled service. A connection does not itself authorize a payment or prove that a transfer was submitted or delivered.

Retention, security, and privacy rights

Records are retained according to service, security, dispute, tax, AML, sanctions, regulatory, legal-hold, and provider requirements. Certain identity, transfer, or compliance records may require retention for five years or another applicable period. We use risk-based administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Subject to applicable law and exceptions, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, withdrawal of consent, marketing opt-out, or an appeal. Identity verification may be required before a request is fulfilled.

Children, international processing, and changes

The service is not intended for children under 13, and money-transfer functionality is intended only for users satisfying the approved age and eligibility rules. Cross-border services may involve processing in the United States and approved destination or service-provider jurisdictions. We may revise this notice when practices, partners, laws, or product features change and will post the revised effective date.

4. Remittance Transfer Disclosures and Rights

Before payment: An applicable transaction disclosure should identify the transfer amount, applicable fees and taxes, total amount, exchange rate, and amount expected to be received, using approved labels and estimates where legally permitted.

After payment: A receipt or combined disclosure should include the transaction details, expected availability date, recipient information, provider contact information, and applicable cancellation, error, and complaint rights.

Transaction-specific controls: The values displayed in the app and receipt for an accepted transfer control over examples on this general webpage.

Cancellation and refund

For a covered remittance transfer, you generally have the right to cancel for a full refund within 30 minutes after payment, unless the funds have already been picked up or deposited into the recipient's account. Contact the provider using the channel printed on the final receipt and provide enough information to identify the transfer. Scheduled transfers may be subject to different cancellation timing under applicable law.

Error resolution

For a covered remittance transfer, submit an oral or written error notice no later than 180 days after the availability date disclosed on the receipt. Include your name and contact information, the recipient's information, the transfer amount, and a description of the suspected error. The responsible provider generally investigates and determines whether an error occurred within 90 days and reports results within three business days after completing the investigation. Available remedies depend on the error and applicable law.

Request the full rights notice

The final provider must make a long-form error-resolution and cancellation notice available upon request using the applicable CFPB model language or substantially similar language.

Request account deletion
